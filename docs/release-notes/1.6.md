GlassCast 1.6
Update Notes:

* Choose whether the skip buttons jump 15 or 30 seconds, in Settings (the icons change to match)
* GlassCast remembers what you were listening to, so the mini player is still there after you close and reopen the app
* Opens faster: casting now loads in the background instead of holding up the launch, which could leave GlassCast stuck on the logo
* The opening animation plays once, and no longer starts over when you use split screen or change the display size
* On Android 12 and newer, the launch screen goes straight into the GlassCast logo instead of showing the app icon first
* If GlassCast crashes, Settings > About now has a crash report you can share. Nothing is sent unless you send it
* Android TV: moving around with the remote is quicker, with no bounce on each press
* Android TV: lists scroll smoothly when you hold down a direction
* Android TV: the background holds still while you move around, so scrolling stays smooth
* Bug fixes and improvements
