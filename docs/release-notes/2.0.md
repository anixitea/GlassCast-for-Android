GlassCast 2.0

If you enjoy GlassCast, you can support it at buymeacoffee.com/theglassmaker

Update Notes:

* A third look, Glass. Turn it on in Settings under Appearance, and pick Frosted, or Liquid for clearer glass. The tab bar, the mini player, the player's controls and the buttons on your pages become real glass that bends what's behind it, with light catching the edges, and the app takes on Apple's layout and colors, set in Inter
* In Glass, scroll down and the mini player becomes a bubble of glass beside the tab bar, with the cover inside and the time running around it. Scroll back up and it comes back
* In Glass, the player's panel is a capsule of glass you pull up into a sheet, play is a disc of glass that changes shape as it plays and pauses, and the scrubber is a rod of glass that swells while you drag it
* Continue Listening at the top of Library: what you're part way through, with the time left
* Your listening history, from the clock button in Library
* Pick your own app icon in Settings under Appearance. There are fifteen, in three sets to match the looks: Original, Sea Glass and Glass. They all keep the same size as the icon you know
* New tab icons, in every look
* New episodes show up faster: refreshing checks six shows at once, the app checks when you open it, and the background check runs every hour
* Swiping an episode now takes a real pull, so scrolling a list doesn't queue episodes or mark them played by accident
* The Appearance choices look the same in every look, and show your accent color and tab bar setting
* Android Auto: Home, Library, Latest and Downloads tabs. Home has Continue Listening and Up Next, Library has your history, episodes show how far in you are, and you can add to Up Next or mark played right from the list
* Android Auto: what you play in the car is still there when you open the app
* Android TV: the Glass look, with buttons, the rail and panels made of glass, and covers that lift and catch the light when you land on them
* Bug fixes and improvements
