GlassCast 1.7

If you enjoy GlassCast, you can support it at buymeacoffee.com/theglassmaker

Update Notes:

* Sign in with Google to keep your shows, progress and bookmarks on every phone you use. It's stored in your own Google account, not on a server
* Or save everything to a backup file in Settings and restore it on a new phone
* Settings is now its own tab, with everything grouped so it's easier to find
* Pick your own accent color in Settings > Appearance
* The mini player goes light or dark to stand out from the page behind it, and fades between the two. The tab bar can do the same if you turn on Adaptive tab bar
* Menus open as sheets that stop below the camera, and hiding, unfollowing or deleting something now asks in a sheet that shows what it is
* Bookmarks: save a spot in an episode and add a memo to it, from the new Bookmarks tab in the player. They show as dots on the progress bar
* The full player works properly with the phone turned sideways
* Tap the episode title in the full player to go to its show
* Headphone next and previous buttons now skip back and forward, so a double tap no longer jumps to another episode. You can change this in Settings > Playback
* Android Auto now has skip back and skip forward buttons
* Shows can download new episodes on their own over Wi-Fi. Turn it on in a show's info
* A home screen widget for what's playing, with play/pause and skip buttons
* Long-press the GlassCast icon for Resume, Latest, Search and Downloads
* Update notes now show their pictures
* Buy me a coffee link in Settings > About
* Bug fixes and improvements
