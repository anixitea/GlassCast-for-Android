GlassCast 1.9

If you enjoy GlassCast, you can support it at buymeacoffee.com/theglassmaker

Update Notes:

* A second look for the whole app, Sea Glass. Turn it on in Settings under Appearance. Pages turn to paper (or deep sea green at night), titles are set in a calm serif, and the buttons, the tab bar and the player's controls become pieces of frosted sea glass that catch the light and press down under your finger
* In Sea Glass, every show brings its own color as glass: the play button is a pebble in the color of the episode's cover, covers sit in a pool of their own color, and show pages and the player are washed with the cover's colors
* In Sea Glass, the mini player's bubble is a pebble of sea glass with the cover inside, and the line around its edge shows how far into the episode you are. It slowly changes shape while it plays
* Sea Glass on Android TV too. Pick it in the TV's Settings under Appearance
* Sign in with Google on Android TV. Scan the QR code with your phone, enter the code, and your library, progress and bookmarks sync to the TV
* On Android TV, the Library opens with Continue listening, the player has bookmarks, and you can reorder Up Next: pick an episode up with its handle (or hold select) and move it with up and down
* In Android Auto, there's a speed button next to the skip buttons, and Continue listening is the first thing you see when you browse
* Your look is saved in backups along with your other settings
* Bug fixes and improvements
