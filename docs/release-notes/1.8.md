GlassCast 1.8

If you enjoy GlassCast, you can support it at buymeacoffee.com/theglassmaker

Update Notes:

* Sign in with Google is here. Sign in from Settings and your shows, progress and bookmarks follow you to every phone you use. They're kept in a hidden folder in your own Google Drive, not on a server
* Fixed GlassCast freezing and saying it isn't responding when you pulled down to refresh
* With the phone turned sideways, the full player no longer shows a block of color on the right, the pull-up panel opens higher, and the sleep timer scrolls so nothing gets cut off
* The tab bar moves more calmly when you switch tabs, and every label fits
* Switches now show clearly when they're off
* Bug fixes and improvements
