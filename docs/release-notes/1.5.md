GlassCast 1.5
Update Notes:

* Refreshed app icon
* Tablet support: in landscape, the player puts the cover on the left with the controls beside it, and podcast pages show the cover on one side with the episodes on the other
* The library fits more shows per row on bigger screens
* Redesigned Downloads: swipe left on an episode to delete it, or tap Select to remove several at once (Select all included)
* Titles in the full player are easier to read on taller screens
* Android TV: fixed getting stuck in the side menu after opening a show from Discover or Search
* Android TV: holding down on an episode now opens its menu right away, no need to let go first
* Android TV: click on a show's description to read all of it
* Bug fixes and improvements
